# Separate production Experiment ownership, consultation, and approval

Every production Flight separates three responsibilities: the Experiment Owner runs the Flight and prepares its evidence, an Independent DS Consultant challenges methods and evidence without approval authority, and the Experiment Review Committee makes the final pass, change, or block decision through Experimentation triage or review. Fixture-only and local development work may use documented, time-bounded role overlap, but that exception never extends to a production Flight. This preserves independent production governance without turning early fixture work into a three-person staffing requirement.
