# Grill Me Decision Log

## Authority legend

- `owner_decision`: explicitly accepted, corrected, or required by the Owner.
- `planner_inference`: a design interpretation or proposed implementation contract that must not be represented as an Owner quote.
- `research_evidence`: a finding from source review or external research; it informs but does not decide the product.

- Organization-specific mappings for causal reviewer, action approver, incident owner, packet recipient, and escalation path.
