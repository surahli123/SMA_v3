M0 · Flight Readiness

M0 review flow Draft

Top-down decision logic from a frozen ExperimentReadContract to one stored readiness state, one typed next safe action, and a sealed packet. Shape carries meaning: ovals start and end, diamonds decide, rectangles act. Nothing below the dashed boundary is authorized by the packet.

Reading the three decisions

  • Authority and contract complete? Missing production authority, a `MISSING` required field, or an unknown owner is a Coverage Gap. Nothing is inferred from old SMA or a dashboard label.
  • Setup and read valid? Identity, assignment/exposure/arm parity, SRM, units and estimator, CUPED mode, source authority, independent recomputation (independence class ≥ transform), authorization. Any material failure or unknown → not_permitted.
  • Preregistered sufficiency reached? Runtime, and if the policy declares it, sample/units, versus preregistered values. Not a power calculation.

What no outcome implies

  • No mutation, deploy, rollback, flag change, message, or publication.
  • No M1 cause claim, no M2 win/loss, no production candidate.
  • No Committee acceptance; decision_grade means the read may be analyzed, not that the Flight passed.
  • A fixture packet (evidence_class = fixture) never demonstrates program M0 capability; a correctly blocked production packet can, once the fixed core executed and the result was independently adjudicated (S11, S13). Program capability and per-Flight analysis_use are two fields, never one.
  • M0 blocking constrains M1 claims per evidence dependency; it does not forbid M1 investigation. An invalid Flight yields a scoped, non-binding recommend_block on its use as decision evidence, never on launch (S9, S10).
  • An unapplied remediation diff, when gated, reaches humans only.
